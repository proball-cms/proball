Three years ago, ProBall was just getting started. A few teams. A group of kids. Coaches with a big idea about what junior basketball could become. Tomorrow, **35 ProBall teams take the court.** That’s a ProBall record, and it’s pretty special to stop for a second and think about how far we’ve come.

Although the number isn’t really what matters. Behind those 35 teams are hundreds of kids getting another opportunity to play basketball, make friends, compete, learn and get better.

## **Some Will Win. Some Won’t.**

That’s basketball. Some of our teams will walk off the court tomorrow celebrating. Others won’t, but every single ProBaller gets the same opportunity t**o play,** compete hard, be a great teammate, make mistakes and learn from them, to have fun, and come back next week a little better.

We’ve written before about how [**one bad basketball game doesn’t define a player**](https://proball.com/blog/what-do-rep-basketball-coaches-actually-look-for.html), and a new season is another reminder of that. Junior basketball is a long journey. One result on one Saturday is just a tiny part of it.

## **From a Few Teams to 35**

We’re incredibly proud of how much ProBall has grown, but we’re even more proud of what that growth represents. More kids playing basketball. More friendships being formed. More opportunities to compete. More young players **building confidence through basketball, a**nd thousands more little moments that these kids will probably remember long after they’ve forgotten the scores.

That’s what we’re building. Not just better basketball players. **Brighter people.**

## **Good Luck, ProBallers ❤️**

So to every player pulling on a ProBall jersey tomorrow: enjoy it. Play hard. Play together. Listen to your coaches. Support your teammates. DON’T worry about being perfect. Just compete.

**35 teams. One ProBall.** Our biggest season yet. Let’s make it our best one too. 💙🏀
