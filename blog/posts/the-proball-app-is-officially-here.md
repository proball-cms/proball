Between school, work, other sports and everything else families have going on, we know how difficult it can be to commit to the same basketball training day every single week. That’s why we’ve always wanted to make training at ProBall as flexible as possible. Now, with the launch of our [**official ProBall App**](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/proball/id6818086060), managing your child’s basketball training is easier than ever.

## **Basketball Training That Fits Around Your Family**

Think of ProBall Academy like a gym membership, but for basketball. Instead of being locked into one training session every week, parents can open the ProBall App, see the available classes and book the sessions that work best for their family.

Monday works this week? Great. Wednesday and Thursday work better next week? No problem. You can even book a session just five minutes before it starts, provided there’s availability.

No complicated forms, no back-and-forth messages and no rearranging your entire week around basketball. **You choose when your child trains, directly from the app.**

## **Your Child Can Train Every Day**

This is the part we’re particularly excited about. At ProBall, we believe young basketball players improve when they have more opportunities to practise, learn and enjoy the game.

Our Academy offers the opportunity to **TRAIN EVERY DAY**, with sessions available across our Sydney training locations.

Not just one training session a week. More opportunities to work on shooting, ball handling, decision-making, confidence and everything else that helps young players develop.

Whether your child is a beginner or already playing competitive basketball, you can choose training sessions that suit their ability, goals and your family’s schedule. Everything is managed through the app, fitting extra basketball into your week is easier than ever.

## **Download the ProBall App Today**

The official ProBall App is now available on both the [**Apple App Store**](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/proball/id6818086060) **and** [**Google Play**](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=ie.zappy.fennec.proball).

Parents can download the app, create their family profile, explore available training sessions and manage bookings directly from their phone.

We’re continuing to make it easier for Sydney families to enjoy basketball without unnecessary complications. **More opportunities to train. More flexibility for parents. More basketball for kids.** That’s what the ProBall App is all about.
