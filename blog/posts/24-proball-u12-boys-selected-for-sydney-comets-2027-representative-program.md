**24 PROBALL BOYS. 16 REP SELECTIONS. 8 DEVELOPMENT PLAYERS. WHAT AN ACHIEVEMENT!** 

We’re incredibly proud to announce that **24 ProBall U12 boys have been selected for the Sydney Comets 2027 Representative Program**, including 16 representative players and 8 Development Players!

This is a fantastic achievement for our young athletes, their families and everyone involved in their basketball development.

## **Congratulations to Our 16 Representative Players!**

Vuk Glamocanin, Lucas Milton, Tristian Ho, Max Bautista, Hugo Byrne, Clancy Page, Luca Paiva Mazziotti, Marcus Robinson, Joel Chen, Cooper Brooks, Boaz Kapar, Jaxon Morris, Liam Semery, Josh Surya, Hunter Trimble and Charlie Hahn.

## **Congratulations to Our 8 Development Players!**

Lincoln Dayoan, Charlie Fell, Oliver Vu, Reuben Chan, Felix Henrici, Otis Kerby, Max Matosin and Jack Laforest.

## **A Proud Moment for the ProBall Community**

Behind every selection are countless training sessions, early mornings, supportive families and young players who genuinely love basketball. Seeing 24 of our boys take this next step into representative basketball is something we’re incredibly proud of.

At ProBall, we work hard to provide young athletes with quality coaching, regular training opportunities and an environment where they can develop their skills and enjoy the game. Watching so many of our players progress into the Sydney Comets representative pathway makes all that work worthwhile.

A massive congratulations to all 24 boys, their families and the coaches who have supported them along the way. We’re honoured to be part of your basketball journey and can’t wait to follow your progress throughout the 2027 season.

**OUR BOYS. OUR PRIDE. THE WORK CONTINUES.**
