Your child’s next favourite basketball coach might have just arrived! We’re excited to welcome **Jessica Love** to the ProBall coaching team.

Jessica currently coaches the **Sydney Comets U18 Boys White representative team**, bringing valuable experience working with competitive junior basketball players. Now, she’ll be sharing that knowledge with our ProBall athletes during training and on game days.

As ProBall continues to grow across Sydney, we’re committed to building a coaching team that gives every child the opportunity to improve, regardless of their experience or ability.

For our younger players, that means learning the fundamentals, developing confidence and discovering what makes basketball so enjoyable. For our more experienced athletes, it means improving their decision-making, refining their skills and learning how to compete at a higher level.

**Great coaching isn’t just about teaching children what to do. It’s about helping them understand how to improve.**

Every coach brings something different to the court, and we’re excited about the experience and perspective Jessica will add to our growing team.

Most importantly, we’re looking forward to seeing the relationships she builds with our athletes and the positive impact she can have on their basketball journeys.

**Welcome aboard, Coach Jess!**  We can’t wait to see you on the court.
