A number of parents have recently asked about trials for 2027 representative basketball teams.

Our local association, Sydney Comets, has now released its trial dates and information for the 2027 season. Full details and registration are available here:

**Sydney Comets:** [https://sydneybasketball.com.au/sydney-comets-junior-representative-trials/](https://sydneybasketball.com.au/sydney-comets-junior-representative-trials/ "https://sydneybasketball.com.au/sydney-comets-junior-representative-trials/")

A number of other nearby associations have also released their 2027 trial information:

**Marrickville Spirit:** [https://www.spiritbasketball.com.au/junior-rep-trials](https://www.spiritbasketball.com.au/junior-rep-trials "https://www.spiritbasketball.com.au/junior-rep-trials")

**Inner West Bulls:** [https://www.innerwestbulls.com/2027-trial-dates-and-registration/](https://www.innerwestbulls.com/2027-trial-dates-and-registration/ "https://www.innerwestbulls.com/2027-trial-dates-and-registration/")

**Northern Suburbs Bears:** [https://www.nsba.com.au/junior-representative-trials-2027](https://www.nsba.com.au/junior-representative-trials-2027 "https://www.nsba.com.au/junior-representative-trials-2027")

Representative basketball is a great option for players who are looking to take the next step and compete at a higher level against teams from across NSW.

If your child is interested in representative basketball and you're unsure whether they're ready to trial, feel free to reach out to info@proball.com and we'll be happy to provide some guidance.
