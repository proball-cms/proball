**Sydney University and Matraville Sports High School. We’re coming in 2027!** We’ve been teasing our Sydney University announcement for the past two weeks, but we had one more surprise waiting.

**ProBall is officially expanding to TWO new Sydney training locations in 2027: Sydney University and Matraville Sports High School!** And we’re not stopping there. More locations are on the way.

## **Bringing Basketball Closer to Sydney Families**

Our goal is simple. We want more kids across Sydney to have the opportunity to play basketball, improve their skills and experience being part of a team.

By expanding into Sydney University and Matraville Sports High School, we’re making it easier for more families to access quality basketball training closer to home.

Whether your child is picking up a basketball for the first time or already playing competitively, ProBall provides opportunities for young players aged 5–15 to develop and enjoy the game.

## **Train Every Day. Choose What Works for You.**

One of the things that makes ProBall different is our **TRAIN EVERY DAY** philosophy. Families aren’t locked into the same training day every week. Through the ProBall app, parents can book suitable, available training sessions across our Sydney locations.

Soccer on Tuesday? School event on Wednesday? No problem. Find another available session that works around your family’s schedule. As we continue expanding, families will have even more options to fit basketball into their week.

## **Don’t Wait Until 2027 to Start!**

Although our new Sydney University and Matraville Sports High School locations are coming in 2027, your child can start playing basketball with ProBall right now.

Join one of our existing Sydney training locations, enjoy your **FIRST 4 WEEKS FREE**, and get on the waitlist for a 2027 competition team.

Competition team places are subject to availability.

**Ready to get started?**

Book an information session and we’ll help you find a suitable training location and explain how to join the 2027 team waitlist.
