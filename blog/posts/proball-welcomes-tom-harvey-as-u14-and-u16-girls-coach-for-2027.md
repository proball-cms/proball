**OUR GIRLS DESERVE GREAT COACHES. AND WE’VE JUST ADDED ANOTHER ONE.** 

We’re excited to welcome **Tom Harvey to ProBall**, where he’ll be coaching our U14 and U16 girls basketball teams in 2027!

Tom is currently the **Sydney Comets U16 Girls Gold coach** and will be entering his **8th season of representative basketball in 2027**. His experience coaching young female athletes makes him a fantastic addition to our growing coaching team.

## **Building a Stronger Future for Girls Basketball in Sydney**

At ProBall, we’re committed to creating more opportunities for girls to play basketball, develop their skills and enjoy being part of a team.

That starts with having experienced coaches who understand the game, know how to develop young players and genuinely care about their progress.

As our girls basketball program continues to grow across Sydney, we’re excited to have Tom helping guide our U14 and U16 teams through the 2027 season.

**Welcome to ProBall, Tom! We can’t wait to see what our girls achieve with you on the sidelines.**
